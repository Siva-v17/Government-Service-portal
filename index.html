Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to visit an office at any point?

No. Identity verification happens through your Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP, and every supporting document is uploaded through the portal. Physical visits are only needed for the small number of services that legally require biometric capture.

How do I know my certificate is genuine?

Every document issued carries a unique reference ID and QR code. Anyone can confirm its authenticity on the Document Verification page without needing a login.

What if my application is rejected?

You'll receive the specific reason in your dashboard and can resubmit corrected documents at no extra cost. Rejections can also be appealed through the Grievance Redress service.

Is there a fee to use the portal itself?

The portal is free to use. You only pay the statutory fee set by the issuing department for a given service, shown before you confirm any application.
