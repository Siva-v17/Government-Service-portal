About the portal

Built to be the easiest way to deal with government — not just the digital version of a form.

Stackly is the unified services layer connecting citizens to Revenue, Municipal Administration, Transport, Health and eleven other departments through one verified account.

OUR MANDATE

Reduce the distance between a citizen and a signature.

Before this portal, an average certificate request touched four counters and took eleven working days. Our mandate is simple: every service that can move online should, and every citizen should be able to see exactly where their request stands at any moment.

  • Verified once, trusted everywhere. A single Aadhaar-linked identity works across all 118 services — no repeated document uploads.
  • Status is not a mystery. Every application shows its current stage, the officer reviewing it, and an expected date.
  • Accessible by design. Built to WCAG 2.1 AA, tested with screen readers, and available in eleven regional languages.
  • Data stays with the department. The portal is a front door, not a database — departments remain the record of truth.
Milestones

How the portal got here

2021

Pilot launch — three districts

Birth and income certificates go live as the portal's first two digitised services, processing under 4,000 applications in the first month.

2022

Statewide rollout

All 38 districts onboarded; trade licences, property tax and grievance redress added to the service catalogue.

2023

Aadhaar-linked single sign-on

Citizens move from department-specific logins to one verified account, cutting average application time by 61%.

2024

Welfare scheme matching

Eligibility engine launched, automatically surfacing pension, scholarship and subsidy schemes a citizen qualifies for.

2026

2.4 crore citizens served

The portal crosses 118 live services and a 97% on-time processing rate across all participating departments.
